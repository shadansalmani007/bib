Who we are

Welcome to Our Family

Building refreshing experiences with quality, technology and care.

01

Bibagry Limited is a Zambian-based FMCG with a strong portfolio of soft drinks. With a multi-million dollar investment into state-of-the-art machinery and technology and extensive technical expertise, Bibagry’s high-performance plant in Kamfinsa is capable of producing more than 36,000 bottles per hour. We employ hundreds of people, and directly support the communities around our plant. Bibagry Limited was founded in 2018 and is wholly owned by Kassco Limited, one of Zambia’s most successful and best-known mining and plant hire contractors, that has been in business since 2005.

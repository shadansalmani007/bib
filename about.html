Our Story

Refreshing lives, building a legacy.

Bibagry Ltd. is a dynamic and innovative manufacturer of non-alcoholic beverages and maize meal, dedicated to delivering high-quality products that meet the diverse needs of consumers in Zambia and beyond.

Established in December 2018, Bibagry has grown into a trusted name in the beverage industry, producing bottled water, carbonated soft drinks, juices, and premium maize meal.

Our Reach

Our Distribution Network

BIB's Beverages delivers freshness across Southern Africa through a strong network of partners and nationwide logistics. From our Zambia headquarters to retail shelves in DRC, Malawi, Namibia, and Botswana, we make sure every bottle arrives cold and ready to enjoy.

Zambia HQ

Central logistics and operations hub serving the region with fast, reliable delivery.

Regional Markets

Growing retail presence in DRC, Malawi, Namibia, and Botswana with consistent shelf availability.

Fresh Delivery

Daily shipments and temperature-controlled handling keep our beverages tasting crisp and delicious.

Partner Network

Strong partnerships with distributors, wholesalers, and retailers ensure fast expansion and local support.
